## Race rule (tested)

Claim file per role: `<claims>/<batch>/<feature>/claim.<role>.json` (relay never overwrites the
peer's file). A cell is skipped when terminal anywhere visible (own output, relayed peer
terminal, FAILED marker, peer claim COMPLETED/FAILED) or when a live peer claim is visible.
**Home priority**: worker_b owns batch_002, never waits, and wins any conflict. The stealer
(worker_a) claims only with a relay heartbeat younger than 300 s, then waits until the relay
has completed three more cycles (pull a → pull b → push a → push b → heartbeat), re-reads,
and abandons if a home claim is visible — BEFORE training. Relay outage: the stealer cannot
claim (stale heartbeat) and abandons an unsettled claim after 25 min; the home role proceeds.
A live claim older than 10 h with no terminal is an orphan and may be re-claimed (recorded).
Nobody is ever killed. Stealer walks the plan in reverse, worker_b forward.
